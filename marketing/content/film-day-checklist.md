# 🎬 Film Day Checklist — Your Founder Story Video
### Phone-only · $0 budget · one relaxed session with your son

> Goal: film ONE main story video (2–3 min) for YouTube + grab a few short clips you
> can cut into Reels/TikTok. Done beats perfect. Your realness is the whole point.

---

## ✅ The day/night before
- [ ] **Review your script** (`youtube-launch-plan.md`) — read it out loud twice, tweak any line that doesn't sound like you.
- [ ] **Make cue cards** — bullet points (not full paragraphs) on sticky notes or your phone notes, to place near the camera.
- [ ] **Charge everything** — phone to 100%, plus your son's phone if he's filming.
- [ ] **Free up phone storage** (video eats space) — delete old stuff or offload photos.
- [ ] **Pick your spot** — a quiet room, tidy background (a plant, shelf, or plain wall works great).
- [ ] **Pick your outfit** — solid colors read best on camera; avoid busy patterns.
- [ ] **Download CapCut** (free) on your phone for editing later.

## 🎥 Gear (all free / stuff you own)
- 📱 Your phone = your camera (use the **back camera** — it's sharper than the selfie one)
- 💡 **Light:** face a **window** in daytime, OR a lamp *behind the phone* pointing at you (never behind you)
- 🎙️ **Sound:** film in a **quiet, soft room** (rugs/curtains reduce echo). Good audio matters more than video.
- 🧱 **Steady:** lean the phone on a stack of books, or grab a cheap stand later — don't hold it by hand for the main talk.

## 📐 Setup (2-minute check before you roll)
- [ ] Phone **horizontal (landscape)** for YouTube; also grab some **vertical** takes for Reels/TikTok.
- [ ] Camera at **eye level** (not looking down at you) — stack books to raise it.
- [ ] You're **framed** from about mid-chest up, a little space above your head.
- [ ] **Look at the camera lens**, not the screen — it feels like eye contact with the viewer.
- [ ] Do a **10-second test clip** → play it back → check you can hear yourself clearly and you're well-lit.

## 🎬 The shot list (film in this order)
**1. The main story (your script)**
- [ ] Film it in **short chunks**, section by section (hook → story → each product → call to action). Way easier than one long take.
- [ ] Do **2–3 takes** of each chunk. Mess up? Pause, smile, start that line again — you'll cut the flubs.
- [ ] Start each take with a **little pause** (makes editing cleaner).

**2. B-roll / clips to cut in (grab these too)**
- [ ] **Screen-record your app** in action (see below) — food search, hitting your goal, the weight chart.
- [ ] A few **"hold up the phone"** shots showing Fuel Tracker (like the great one your son already took!).
- [ ] Your **book cover** on screen or in hand.
- [ ] The **kit / Gumroad page** on screen.
- [ ] A couple of **candid smiles / b-roll** of you at your laptop "building."

**3. Screen recordings (free, built in)**
- [ ] iPhone: Settings → Control Center → add **Screen Recording**, then swipe down and tap record.
- [ ] Android: swipe down → **Screen Recorder** tile.
- [ ] Record: opening Fuel Tracker, searching a food, logging it, the summary/weight tab.

## 😊 While filming — keep it easy
- Talk like you're explaining it **to a friend**, not performing.
- **Smile before you start** — it warms up your voice.
- Energy slightly *higher* than feels natural (camera flattens it).
- It's OK to mess up — just pause and redo that line. Bloopers get cut.
- Take **water breaks**; don't rush. This is supposed to be a little fun with your son. 💚

## ✂️ After — edit in CapCut (free)
- [ ] Import your clips → arrange the story in order → trim the flubs and long pauses.
- [ ] Add **auto-captions** (CapCut does this in one tap — most people watch on mute!).
- [ ] Drop in **soft background music** (CapCut's free library or YouTube Audio Library) — keep it low under your voice.
- [ ] Add simple **text pop-ups**: product names + "Free 7-day trial," "$19.99," "$7.99," your links.
- [ ] Export in **1080p**.

## 📤 Where it goes
- [ ] **YouTube:** the full 2–3 min story (use the title/description from `youtube-launch-plan.md`).
- [ ] **Reels / TikTok / IG:** cut a punchy **30–60 sec vertical** version (the hook + one product + CTA).
- [ ] **Facebook:** share the story video to your warm audience.
- [ ] Put your **link (or Linktree)** in the caption/bio.

## 🌟 The golden rules
1. **Done > perfect.** Your first video won't be your best — post it anyway. You improve by doing.
2. **You are the magic.** Authentic and a little imperfect beats slick and impersonal every time.
3. **Batch it.** Film everything in one session so you're not setting up over and over.
4. **Have fun with your son.** That warmth will show on camera — and it's the good stuff. 🎥💛

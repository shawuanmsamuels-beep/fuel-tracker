# Story-style voiceover — Ship It Without Code

> A different flavor from the direct-sell script: this one uses the punchy,
> curiosity-driven "build-up then reveal" cadence (short lines, concrete numbers,
> a "notice something" turn) — the same style as the social-circles/Dunbar piece.
>
> Two things below:
> 1. **The reusable prompt** — paste into ChatGPT/Claude to generate this style for
>    ANY product (swap the facts).
> 2. **The finished starter-kit script** it produces — clean, TTS-ready for ElevenLabs.

---

## 1) THE REUSABLE PROMPT (copy/paste into an AI)

```
You are writing a short spoken-word VOICEOVER SCRIPT for a faceless/explainer-style
video. Use this specific cadence and structure:

STYLE RULES
- Short, punchy sentences. Often fragments. One idea per line.
- Open with a curiosity hook — a gap, a mystery, or a bold claim — NOT a greeting.
- Use concrete, specific numbers and vivid details, never vague adjectives.
- Build a structure the listener can picture (stages, rings, steps, layers).
- Include ONE "notice something" reveal near the end that snaps the structure into a
  simple pattern (this is the payoff — make it satisfying).
- Land on the product naturally as the answer, then a soft, confident CTA.
- Honest and anti-hype. No income promises, no fake urgency, no "guru" voice.
- Written to be SPOKEN: spell out all numbers and prices as words (e.g. "nineteen
  ninety-nine", "ten steps"), so text-to-speech reads them smoothly.
- Output ONLY the words to be spoken — no stage directions, headers, or timestamps.
- Length: about 250–350 spoken words (roughly a 2-minute video). Keep it under 5,000
  characters so it fits one ElevenLabs generation.

THE PRODUCT (swap these facts for any product)
- Name: Ship It Without Code
- What it is: a kit of 50 AI prompts, in order, that take a non-coder from "idea" to
  a live, paid product. Organized in 5 parts of 10 prompts each:
  1) Find & Validate Your Idea, 2) Build It, 3) Fix It When It Breaks,
  4) Launch & Get Your First Customer, 5) Automate & Scale Without Burning Out.
- Also includes: the real build story of the app I shipped, and 3 worksheets
  (Idea Validator, Build Tracker, Launch Checklist).
- Price/terms: $19.99, instant download, 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Credibility: written by someone with 20 years in banking and zero coding
  background, who used these exact prompts to build and ship a real paid web app.
- The core insight to reveal: the barrier isn't skill or talent — it's SEQUENCE.
  Knowing the order of moves. (Fifty prompts, five stages, ten each, one at a time.)
- CTA target: the link is "below / in the description."

Now write the voiceover script.
```

## 3. FILMING NOTES ($0 budget, phone only)

- Long-form: prop the phone against a stack of books or a mug for the on-camera parts
  — no tripod needed. Face a window for free natural light, don't shoot with it behind
  you.
- For all "on-screen" cues, use your phone's built-in screen recorder to capture the
  kit PDF/pages and, briefly, Fuel Tracker in use. Two apps recorded natively on one
  phone is enough — no fancy screen-capture software required.
- Short: film vertical (phone upright), keep every cut under 3–4 seconds, and caption
  the whole thing on-screen (most people watch Shorts muted). CapCut (free, mobile) is
  enough to cut this together and auto-caption it.
- Keep the Fuel Tracker cutaways brief in both versions — a flash, not a tour. This
  video's job is to sell the kit; Fuel Tracker is only there as proof it's real.
- Don't re-film for small stumbles — pause, breathe, restart the sentence, and cut the
  gap out in editing. Natural, slightly imperfect delivery reads as more trustworthy
  for a direct-sell video like this one.
- Pin the kit link as the first comment on both uploads (Shorts especially benefit from
  a pinned link since the description is easy to miss).

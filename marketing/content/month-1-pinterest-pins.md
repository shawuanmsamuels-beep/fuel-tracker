## How to use this batch
- Post ~1 pin/day (or 2 on batch days) rather than all 16 at once — Pinterest rewards steady activity.
- Use the exact **Pin Title** as the on-platform title field, not just image text.
- Always add the destination URL in the pin's **link** field, not only in the description.
- New boards introduced this month: **Meal Planning** (Fuel Tracker) and **Digital Product Ideas** (Ship It Without Code). Create these alongside your existing 6 boards.

## 4) When you add Fuel Tracker later (notes)

- Sell the **recurring** angle hard to affiliates — they earn on every monthly
  rebill, which is far more attractive than a one-time payout.
- Commission on subscriptions is usually lower % (e.g. 30–40%) but pays monthly.
- You'll need Fuel Tracker's checkout routed through ClickBank — a separate build.
